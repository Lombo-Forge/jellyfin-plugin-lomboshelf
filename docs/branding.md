## The rule

**The Lombo L is in every icon.** Each app has its own seal, and a small mark of its own sits in the open corner of the L.
Each app also gets one wordmark trick (Tier's letters step down, Shrink's get smaller from S to K, Shelf's F leans).

## Colours and type

Background `#0d0a08`, surface `#1a140f`, borders `#2c231a` and `#443528`, text `#f4ede3` and `#b3a696`, ember gradient
`#ffb347` to `#ff6a1a` to `#e23d0f`. Headings and wordmarks: Cinzel (SIL Open Font License). Body text: Geist.
(These are the Lombo-Forge brand colours. The plugin's own theme colours are separate settings; see docs/design.md.)

## LomboShelf's mark

Five books standing on the foot of the L in the corner, in the ember colours, the last one leaning. Wordmark: the F of SHELF leans
(the SVG asks for a 9 degree tilt with the `rotate` attribute; the renderer used for the PNG ignored it, so the PNG shows an upright F).
Files: `assets/icon.svg`, `banner.svg`, `social-preview.svg` and their PNGs, rendered with the command in the workspace's
`forge-branding` skill.

## What to avoid

No Tolkien names, scripts, rings or symbols. No logos, names or colours of other products. "For Jellyfin" is fine in a
description; never imply the Jellyfin project made the app.
